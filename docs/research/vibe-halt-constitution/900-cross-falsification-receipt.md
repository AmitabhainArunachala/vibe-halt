## Corrections made load-bearing

### 1. Validity, assessment, governability, and investment are orthogonal

The corrected algebra is a product, not one overloaded green/red state:

```text
AttemptValidity = Valid | Invalid(IntegrityViolation)
Assessment<Action> = HALT | PROCEED | UNKNOWN
Governability = GOVERNABLE(BoundaryWitnesses) | UNGOVERNED
StageDecision = Advance | Hold | Reorient | Kill | Invalid
```

A malformed, wrong-subject, wrong-digest, invalid-signature, wrong-schema, or
contradictory object makes an attempt `Invalid` and produces **no target
assessment**. An unavailable but otherwise uncompromised mandatory object can
leave a valid attempt at `UNKNOWN`. `HALT` is reserved for a blocking finding or
failed mandatory precondition on the exact valid subject
([000:284–312](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L284),
[101:437–480](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L437),
[103:198–219](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L198)).

Engine evidence states do not equal the action-typed policy projection, and
neither authority nor a stage-investment decision may coerce modality
([000:284–334](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L284),
[106:22–35](./106-falsification-economic-kill-matrix.md#L22),
[106:249–265](./106-falsification-economic-kill-matrix.md#L249)).

### 2. The first campaign owes both shaking and binding

The campaign cannot satisfy “Antithesis-like” behavior with a wrapper-level
binding check alone. It has two independently required, identity-coupled
obligations:

1. **Behavioral shaker:** find one source-derived Dharma mutant through the real
   CPython mutation-to-review path, independently replay it, and shrink it
   without changing `FaultClassId` or `FindingId`.
2. **Admission integrity:** bind a human-supplied control candidate to that same
   finding and reject same-path/different-bytes substitution and the remaining
   authority/identity attacks before consequence.

The exact mutant, property, palette, target revision, and numerical thresholds
remain human-frozen campaign inputs, not findings invented after execution
([101:46–56](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L46),
[101:352–382](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L352),
[000:753–785](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L753)).

The corrected chronology has two distinct MAP obligations:

```text
baseline MAP
  -> find/replay/shrink named behavioral fault
  -> materialize and freeze exact candidate + signed receipt
  -> candidate MAP while holdout remains hidden
  -> public replay
  -> hidden fresh/widened re-shake
  -> role-scoped observations and independent replay
  -> evidence closure
  -> action-typed admission
```

Baseline MAP freezes the initial accounting set; candidate MAP recomputes it
over clones of the exact immutable candidate after materialization and before
holdout reveal. Treatment narration cannot substitute for either map
([101:384–435](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L384)). R0/R1 therefore require
both real-target observability and the identity-coupled treatment/admission loop;
A1 reach alone is not the constitutional proof
([107:158–170](./107-antithesis-grade-path.md#L158),
[108:577–592](./108-governable-refinery.md#L577)).

The initial `CooperativeD2` mechanism campaign may honestly produce a signed
`HALT` or bounded `UNKNOWN`, but it cannot produce a review permit while all 29
subprocess channels remain open. Only a fresh later campaign under a
human-ratified eligible envelope may add the permit criterion
([101:477–486](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L477),
[101:622–634](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L622)).

### 3. Materialization and subject identity are attempt-bound

A path or marker file is not subject identity. The materializer freezes the
canonical base, canonical diff, candidate tree, path set, clean-state checks,
runtime/dependency closure, role, and immutable snapshot into a signed
`MaterializationReceiptId`. An observed mismatch is `Invalid`; an identity that
cannot be observed is an open obligation on a valid exact subject
([101:266–300](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L266)).

`SubjectId` carries that receipt and the produced artifact identity. A
byte-identical rematerialization is a new signed observation, not retroactive
authority to swap workspaces inside an existing attempt. The evidence closure
must include every materialization receipt referenced by a subject
([000:396–412](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L396),
[104:140–152](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L140),
[104:231–241](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L231)).

### 4. Campaign and admission identities form an acyclic graph

The audit removed both pre-run/post-run identity cycles:

```text
CampaignSpecId
  -> ordered HoldoutCommitment statement ids
  -> CampaignId
  -> role-scoped ObservationClosure + ObservationAttestation
  -> EvidenceClosureId
  -> AdmissionPayloadId<Action>
  -> ordered JudgeAttestations
  -> AdmissionRecordId
       +-> AdmissionQuorumWitness<Policy>
       +-> GovernabilityGateWitness<Action, Scope, Policy> when satisfied
```

`CampaignSpecId` freezes the campaign contract without a future holdout
commitment or produced evidence. Holdout statements bind that spec; only their
ordered identities construct `CampaignId`. Runners and replayers sign their own
observations, never a future verdict. Judges sign identical action-typed
`AdmissionPayloadId` bytes; the record and quorum witness are assembled only
afterward. No object signs bytes containing its own future signature
([000:355–415](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L355),
[104:95–138](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L95),
[104:217–239](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L217)).

The admission payload carries `Action`, `Assessment<Action>`, the pure
governability projection and decision, and the quorum-policy identity. Action is
not erased into a generic `PROCEED`; the proposed permit is indexed specifically
to `HumanReview` and binds its exact admission and parameterized quorum witnesses
([102:61–140](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L61)).

### 5. Governability is total, fail-closed, and still unratified

`GOVERNABLE` remains accounting closure, never a synonym for clean, correct,
safe, proved, or `PROCEED`. Whether a structurally bounded `UNKNOWN` may coexist
with the public label remains a human decision in #112/#114
([000:578–611](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L578),
[108:13–46](./108-governable-refinery.md#L13)).

The corrected gate is total over the policy question:

```text
GovernabilityGateDecision<Action, Scope, Policy> =
    RequiredAndGovernable(ProjectionId)
  | RequiredButUngoverned(ProjectionId, UnboundedBlindSpots)
  | NotRequired(ProjectionId, RatifiedPolicyClauseId)
```

`RequiredButUngoverned` preserves attributable signed `HALT`/`UNKNOWN` evidence
but derives neither a gate witness nor a permit. `NotRequired` must cite a
ratified action-specific clause and cannot silently omit the projection. The
projection itself contains no quorum witness; only verification of the completed
admission record may derive the gate witness, avoiding an authority cycle
([000:116–147](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L116),
[102:125–135](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L125),
[108:403–466](./108-governable-refinery.md#L403)).

Finite discovery still cannot establish every behavior of arbitrary software.
The refinery accounts for declared, discovered, and platform-mandatory path
classes; a possible consequential blind spot without a structural bound blocks
`GOVERNABLE` itself
([108:84–145](./108-governable-refinery.md#L84),
[000:613–639](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L613)).

### 6. Treatment and DISTILL never gain authorship privilege

Treatment is a product function and an untrusted guest of admission. It has zero
weight in admission, scope ratification, trust roots, holdout, or appeal for its
own candidate, and it cannot write judge/oracle/evidence/kernel/holdout surfaces
([000:220–238](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L220),
[000:530–564](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L530),
[105:14–41](./105-nonsovereign-authority.md#L14)).

Any patch, guard, model implementation, isolation kernel, or consequence-routing
change produced by `DISTILL-CODE` is a new untrusted `SubjectId`. It must re-enter
at `FREEZE/MAP/CONTRACT` and undergo the full independent fresh/hidden re-shake.
Only analysis or proof of a residue already present in the frozen, mapped,
re-shaken subject may proceed without adding code, and it still needs a
separately admitted correspondence/bypass record
([000:179–202](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L179),
[108:366–390](./108-governable-refinery.md#L366)).

### 7. Quorum and authority stay parameterized and outside modality

The evidence research recommends 2-of-3 offline policy root, 2-of-2 judges, two
curators, two witnesses, and two storage replicas as a settlement-grade topology;
it does **not** ratify that topology. One judge cannot manufacture permission
under 2-of-2, but either can deny liveness and force `UNKNOWN`, an effective veto
where normal action requires `PROCEED`. Actual custody, threshold, independence,
availability, and sovereignty tradeoffs remain #110
([103:13–45](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L13),
[103:141–175](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L141),
[000:1174–1186](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L1174)).

The minimum institutional shape separates scope, treatment, runner, admission,
transparency/monitoring, and external decision authority. A single operator with
separated keys is a weaker disclosed mechanism grade, not institutional
independence
([105:14–37](./105-nonsovereign-authority.md#L14),
[105:147–170](./105-nonsovereign-authority.md#L147)).

Signatures establish attributable speech, not truth. An external authority may
record a distinct, scoped `ActAnyway` disposition if its own constitution allows
that exception; it cannot overwrite the underlying `UNKNOWN` or lift it to
`PROCEED`
([103:227–240](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L227),
[105:106–127](./105-nonsovereign-authority.md#L106)).

### 8. Reach, determinism, containment, and artifact identity do not cast

The three execution envelopes are different trust shapes, not rungs whose
evidence can be relabeled:

- `CooperativeD2` reaches the native target but cannot support a positive
  admission while a mandatory claim depends on open channels;
- `NativeInterposed` preserves native-artifact fidelity, but determinism does not
  make the supervisor a security boundary; and
- `CapabilityClosed` may remove ambient capabilities, but the WASI artifact is a
  new subject and capability closure does not imply determinism.

Evidence is indexed by artifact, envelope, controller set, and campaign; a
cross-envelope result needs an explicit correspondence claim and inherits no
stronger grade
([104:11–25](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L11),
[104:37–45](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L37),
[104:62–82](./104-execution-envelopes.md#L62)). A disposable worktree supplies
recoverability, not containment; the campaign must freeze its own credential,
network/data, isolation, resource, and write-boundary policy
([101:308–334](./101-dharma-promotion-campaign.md#L308)).

“Antithesis-grade” therefore remains a measured vector over real-target reach,
control, exploration, replay, minimization, and economic yield—not an analogy or
badge. A1 is observatory reach; stronger claims require fresh evidence at later
rungs
([107:5–20](./107-antithesis-grade-path.md#L5),
[107:61–74](./107-antithesis-grade-path.md#L61)).

### 9. Proof certifies the residue, not the mass or the institution

The proposed first theorem is confined to a pure, total, fail-closed issuance
function for one exact human-review candidate. It proves exact binding,
`PROCEED`-only issuance under the frozen policy, review-only capability,
non-escalation, and deterministic encoding; it does not prove the repair, Dharma,
the path map, oracle adequacy, cryptography, institutional independence, or
external compliance
([102:13–37](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L13),
[102:167–283](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L167),
[102:285–310](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L285)).

`NoConsequenceConstructor` is API confinement, not a theorem that every caller
will honor the bytes. Parser fuzzing, mutation checks, compile-fail capability
tests, an independent campaign, and an end-to-end consequence-router negative
remain distinct obligations. The published TCB still includes theorem/policy

[102:337–362](./102-promotion-permit-residue.md#L337)). Verus is a research
recommendation only; #109 still chooses the court, TCB, timebox, and revival

## Caveats preserved rather than “solved”

The audit did not close these claims:

- accepted main does not yet govern a foreign target end to end, sign
  settlement-grade evidence, implement native interposition or a
  capability-closed backend, or turn its current D2 runs into D1;
- finite simulation, coverage, consensus, signatures, transparency, formal
  syntax, or a proof of one residue cannot prove the repository or world;
- Vibe Halt cannot promise autonomous repair of every repository;
- no proof court, execution backend, trust topology, holdout protocol, public
  `GOVERNABLE` atom, or external exception policy has been ratified; and
- this corpus implements no cryptographic topology and authorizes no code or
  consequence.

Those are explicit draft non-claims
([000:1121–1148](./000-evolution-constitution-draft.md#L1121)). The evidence
research additionally preserves common-mode and liveness limits: a curator can
leak the pool, one 2-of-2 judge can deny liveness, shared organizations or
infrastructure weaken independence, transparency detects rather than prevents
misstatement, and signatures cannot validate an oracle
([103:459–482](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L459),
[103:524–544](./103-evidence-sovereignty.md#L524)).
